Colour

Three logo pastels — sun, lavender, sage — each grown into a nine-step scale so the soft tones stay decorative while deeper steps do the working UI jobs. A coastal cast of sea blue and terracotta supports them, on a warm cream canvas. Sage is the primary action colour.

The bento grid

Navigation lives in the cards, not a menu. A four-column grid with a fixed row unit, dense auto-flow, and modular tile spans builds a composed, magazine-like rhythm. Tiles are draggable — the board feels arranged by hand. On a phone it folds to one or two columns, preserving the bento feel.

05

Buttons & links

Soft pills in the warm palette. Sage is the primary action; secondary is a quiet outline; text links pair an arrow that nudges on hover. Clear, never aggressive — booking should feel like an invitation.

06

Image style

Warm, natural light; golden-hour tones; calm, lived-in spaces. Photos sit in rounded frames, or full-bleed under a bottom scrim for legible text. Where a photo isn't placed yet, a soft coastal wash with a dashed frame marks the slot — an honest, inviting placeholder.

07

Iconography

Lucide line icons at ~2px stroke — even weight and rounded caps that echo the logo's soft geometry. They usually sit in a tinted rounded tile (brand -100 background, -700 icon). No emoji as icons; only the occasional ♥ or ☀ as a warm sign-off.

08

Spacing

An 8px base grid, used generously. The brand breathes — sections rest on ~104px of vertical rhythm, prose stays under 68 characters, and negative space does as much work as the ink.

  • Section rhythm — 104px between page sections (--section-y).
  • Readable prose capped at 68ch; documents at 760px.
  • Card padding 24–32px; tile gaps 14–20px.
  • Touch targets never below 44px.
09

Bento component vocabulary

The reusable cards the board is built from. Each fills its tile and reads the page's accent and theme, so the same widget feels coastal in Saranda and urban in Prishtina.

10

Mobile behaviour

The bento doesn't become a boring list on a phone — it folds to a one- or two-column stack that keeps the modular rhythm: a feature card, paired fact tiles, a tall photo, a dark accent. Order is preserved from the desktop arrangement.

One column below 560px, two from 560–820px. Drag is disabled on touch in favour of native scroll; every card stays tappable with a 44px minimum target. The sticky header collapses to the logo mark and a single back affordance.
